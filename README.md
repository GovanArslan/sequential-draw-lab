#Sequential Draw Lab

The Sequential Draw Lab models a sequential-draw game. In the initial launch case, two tokens are drawn in order, without replacement, out of a bag. 

The payoff is based on the colour pattern, regardless of order:
- two red tokens amount for a payoff of +3;
- one red and one blue token amount for a payoff of +1;
- two blue tokens result in a payout of -2

The project will first provide exact enumeration and analysis, then later add controlled random sampling.
